## What each check can and cannot tell you

- **Key facts (code):** the answer contains the specific facts. Catches wrong or vague answers. Cannot catch a right fact wrapped in a wrong claim.
- **Citation (code):** the answer cites at least one document and only documents that were retrieved.
- **Faithfulness (judge model):** every claim is supported by the retrieved notes. Cannot tell whether the answer is correct. The judge is hand-checked with `--agreement`.
- **Abstention (code):** on out-of-scope questions the answer is exactly the abstention phrase.
